Warning. Glyphix TV displays full-screen flashing lights, rapidly changing colours and moving patterns. These can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy and may cause dizziness, nausea, headaches or eye discomfort. Do not use if you or anyone nearby is sensitive to flashing light. If you have a history of seizures or are unsure, seek medical advice before use. Keep the room well lit, sit well back, start at low brightness and take regular breaks. Warn everyone nearby and make sure they agree before starting. Stop immediately if anyone feels unwell. Keep the remote within reach. Press Back at any time to stop. Continuing does not make these effects safe.
